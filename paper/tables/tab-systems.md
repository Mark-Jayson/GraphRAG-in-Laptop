|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vector` | 3052 mW | 0.0422 | 0.0155 | 1.55 |

The graph arm costs **1.51x** the dense arm's energy per query at the medians above (1.55x as measured) -- the 1.42x latency gap plus a 6% power premium.

**The ratio is the quotable figure, not the absolute.** The measurement window ran at 2.6x the generation latency of the table above (the machine had been under sustained load for nine hours and the GPU was thermally throttled), so the measured per-query energy corresponds to a slower operating point. Both arms slowed by the same factor, so the ratio survives; the 'at Table med.' column applies the measured draw to the reported medians and is an estimate.
