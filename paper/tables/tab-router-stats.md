# Table: routing significance (tab-router-stats)

Generated by `scripts/make_tables.py` from `results/analysis/router_significance.json`. Every policy is compared against always-dense on the same 500 questions. ΔEM is the paired difference in exact match; the interval is a 95% percentile bootstrap over 100,000 resamples of the per-question difference (seed 20260814); b/c are the discordant counts (questions the policy alone answers correctly / always-dense alone answers correctly) and feed an exact two-sided McNemar test, which is the p-value corrected in the last two columns. Subscripts are the multiplicity family size: Holm₂₀ corrects across every test in this table, Holm₆ across the six cascade tests alone; the deployable family (m=15, dropping the two oracles) falls between them. Holm is reported rather than Bonferroni because it controls the same familywise error rate with uniformly more power; the full grid of both corrections across all three families is in `tab-router-stats-full.csv`. Policies marked * are upper bounds, not deployable. `random` is a single seeded draw here against Table 5's 50-draw mean, a difference of up to 1.8 EM.

**Both cascade directions survive correction on 2Wiki and MuSiQue, and dense-first survives on all three benchmarks under every family definition reported here.** Graph-first does not clear correction on HotpotQA at m=20 (Holm 0.106, Bonferroni 0.177), where it clears only within the cascade family (0.009). **No other deployable policy survives any correction on any benchmark**: the heuristic's +2.8 on 2Wiki, the type-oracle's +3.8 there and always-graph's +4.2 on MuSiQue are nominally significant at p < .05 and all fail once the family is accounted for. Note that the dense-first tests are one-sided by construction — b/c of 19/0, 38/0 and 26/0 — because the policy returns the dense answer unchanged unless dense refused, and a refusal is scored wrong; the effect size and its interval carry that result, not the p-value (§6.3).
